I worked as a freelance Unity developer on this VR project as part of a cross-functional team for [Calico](https://wearecalico.co.uk/). Together we produced an 360-video headset VR experience aimed at helping night-time econonmy staff, such as bouncers and bar staff, understand the fem experience of nightlife spaces like bars and club.

Here's how Calico describe the project: 

"Building on the success of CurfewVR, this new project takes our empathy-led approach directly into bars, pubs, nightclubs, and music venues, supporting staff to better understand and respond to gendered violence and harassment. Our goal is to facilitate culture change and promote a victim-centred approach to safety in nightlife spaces."
